;; Bot messages, English (for the demo). Edit the text right here, then restart the server.
;; "=== name" starts a message. The lines below it are its text (it may run over several lines).
;; Lines starting with ;; are notes, the bot ignores them.
;; Words in { } are numbers the bot fills in: keep them, or drop them, but do not add new ones.
;; Do not rename the "=== name" lines. Messenger buttons are 20 characters at most.

;; ===== After a photo: the bot sends the numbered picture, then these lines joined into one message =====

;; Line 1 when some shrimp are left out. {n} counted, {k} left out.
=== photo.count_left_out
Measured {n} shrimp ({k} left out)

;; Line 1 when none are left out.
=== photo.count
Measured {n} shrimp

;; Line 2. {cm} average length (cm), {size} shrimp per kg.
=== photo.size
Average length {cm} cm · about {size} shrimp/kg

;; Line 2 when the size is outside the size table.
=== photo.size_outside
Average length {cm} cm · size is outside the table (60 to 140 mm)

;; Added when the temporary size table or a rough estimate is used.
=== photo.rough
Rough estimate, may be off by 20%.

;; Added when many shrimp overlap (also used in the "too few" message below).
=== photo.crowded
Many shrimp overlap in the photo. Spread them out and retake it for a more accurate measurement.

;; Line 1 when fewer shrimp are counted than the minimum. {n} counted, {min} the minimum.
=== photo.too_few
Only {n} shrimp counted, at least {min} needed. Not saved.

;; Line 2 of the "too few" message (when the photo is not crowded).
=== photo.too_few_hint
Lay the shrimp so they do not touch and retake the photo, or type "choose 3" to add one.

=== photo.no_reference
I can't see a ruler or card in the photo, so I can't work out mm. Place a ruler next to the shrimp and retake it.

=== photo.no_shrimp
I can't see any shrimp in the photo. Lay them flat on a light background and retake it.

;; After tapping Save sample.
=== photo.saved
Sample saved.

;; Save was tapped but the result cannot be saved yet.
=== photo.not_saved
Not saved.

;; ===== Choosing shrimp =====

;; When the shrimp numbers are wrong (for example "1 9" with only 6 shrimp). {n} shrimp in the photo.
=== choose.bad_number_list
Shrimp numbers must be from 1 to {n}. Example: 1 3 5.

;; When "choose" or "remove" comes with wrong numbers.
=== choose.bad_number_cmd
Shrimp numbers must be from 1 to {n}. Example: choose 1 3 or remove 2.

=== left_out.none
No shrimp are left out.

;; Sent after the picture of the left-out shrimp (orange). {example} looks like "1 3".
=== left_out.caption
These shrimp are left out (orange), with their length in cm. Type "choose {example}" to add them to the result.

;; ===== General =====

;; A number or "yes" arrives when no photo is waiting.
=== misc.no_pending
Send a photo of the shrimp tray first. I will measure it, then reply "yes" to save.

;; "cancel" while a result is waiting.
=== misc.cancelled
Discarded the last result.

;; The photo waited too long (30 minutes) and was dropped.
=== misc.expired
That photo has expired, please send it again.

=== misc.download_failed
Could not download the photo. Please send it again.

;; The segmentation model could not process the photo.
=== misc.cannot_process
Could not process this photo. Please retake it.

;; Unexpected error.
=== misc.sorry
Something went wrong, please try again.

;; ===== "Status": these lines are joined into one message, a line shows only when there is data for it =====

=== status.empty
No measurements yet. Send a photo of the shrimp tray to start.

;; First line. {date} the day (for example 02/11), {data} the length and size parts below.
=== status.latest_date_data
Latest measurement {date}: {data}.

=== status.latest_data
Latest measurement: {data}.

=== status.latest_date
Latest measurement {date}.

=== status.latest
Latest measurement.

;; Two pieces put into {data}, separated by a comma.
=== status.part_length
average length {cm} cm

=== status.part_size
about {size} shrimp/kg

;; Slow growth. {growth} mm per day, {threshold} the limit the farmer set.
=== status.slow
Shrimp are growing slowly: about {growth} mm per day, below the {threshold} you set. This is only a hint, you decide.

=== status.growing
Shrimp are still growing: about {growth} mm per day.

=== status.unclear
Cannot tell yet: shrimp size varies up and down, more samples are needed.

=== status.not_enough
Not enough data to compare with the last measurement.

;; {size} shrimp/kg, {price} VND per kg.
=== status.price
At {size} shrimp/kg, the price is about {price} VND/kg (default price table, yours may differ).

;; {size} the next size class, {gain} percent better price, {days} is the "about ... days" text below (may be empty).
=== status.next_class
The {size} shrimp/kg class usually sells for about {gain}% more{days}.

=== status.next_class_days
 (about {days} days left if the shrimp grow as just measured, very rough)

=== status.breakeven
Feed only breaks even if shrimp grow more than about {mm} mm per day (power, labour and risk not counted).

=== status.stall_cost
One day without growth costs about {cost} VND in feed.

;; Last line if a dashboard link is configured.
=== status.chart_link
See the chart: {link}

;; ===== Help =====

=== help.text
How to use
1. Send one photo of the shrimp tray to measure it.
2. Tap "yes" to save the sample.
3. Type "choose 1 3" to add shrimp.
4. Type "remove 1 3" to take shrimp out.
5. Type 1 3 5 to count only these shrimp.
6. Type "show left out" to see the shrimp that are left out.
7. Type "cancel" to discard the last result.
8. Type "status" to see the measurements so far.
9. Type "setup" to change the pond name and feed price.
10. Type "help" to see this again.
