# Vietnam whiteleg shrimp farm-gate price by size (con/kg)

Researched: 2 Nov 2026. Question: what is the farm-gate price (VND/kg) of whiteleg shrimp by size 20 to 100 con/kg, how steep is the step per size, and who publishes a table regularly?

## Answer
- Latest full size table: Tepbac national median, 02/11/2026, mostly Ca Mau buyers [1]: 20 = 200k, 30 = 155k, 40 = 132k, 50 = 120k, 60 = 113k, 70 = 110k, 80 = 107k, 90 = 104k, 100 = 100k VND/kg.
- Price falls steeply at the big end and flattens at the small end: 20 to 30 con/kg loses about 45k, 40 to 50 loses 12k, 60 to 70 loses 3k (derived).
- Day to day noise is large: Tepbac 31/10 gave 40 = 130k, 50 = 125k, 60 = 115k, 100 = 93k [1]. Treat any single value as plus or minus 5k.
- Last 3 months: up 8 to 22 percent depending on size (Tepbac 90-day change on 31/10/2026) [1].
- Last 12 months: no clean series found. Spot points suggest large sizes are down about 15 to 20 percent and size 100 is about flat (low confidence, see section below).
- Default table for the app: use the 02/11/2026 column above, rounded, and let the farmer overwrite it.

## 12-month movement (low confidence)
- Tepbac per-size series only starts 2026-10-07 [2], so no 12-month chart exists there.
- Size 100: Dak Lak 100k end Oct 2025 [6] against 93-100k now [1]: about flat to -7% (different province, derived).
- Size 20: Ca Mau 250k end Oct 2025 [6] against 200-207k now [1]: about -17 to -20% (derived).
- A Tomota weekly post for week 42/2026 says prices are still lower than a year ago for most sizes [9], snippet only.
- A VASEP line says Ca Mau size 30 fell to 152k in Oct 2025 [6], but the text does not say species, so it is not used.

## Where to find an updated table

- VASEP weekly provincial lists (Dong Thap, Dak Lak, Khanh Hoa; coarse size bands): https://vasep.com.vn/gia-thuy-san [5]
- AgroMonitor / thitruongtom.com, daily factory buying price in the Mekong Delta for 30-80 con/kg, content mostly behind a login: https://www.thitruongtom.com/ [10]
- Tomota weekly video and Facebook summaries (secondary): [9]
- Green Bio feed company price board: https://tapdoangreenbio.vn/gia-thuy-san/ [11]

## Unknown or conflicting
- Region: Tepbac "national median" is mostly Ca Mau; central provinces pay more (Quang Tri 30-40 con/kg = 240k end Oct 2025 [6]).
- Dong Nai report gives 180-220k for 30-40 con/kg [7], far above Tepbac (132-160k). Probably retail or market price; do not use as farm gate.
- Facebook posts dated 31/10/2026 repeat the Dec 2024 Long An numbers [8] under a new date. Check the original date before trusting a copied post.
- Tepbac size 50 shows +22% in 30 days while size 60 shows -14.8% on 31/10: thin sample and single-day quotes. Tepbac size 40 page says +1.5% in a month and the list page says -1.1%.
- Quotes are for live or fresh whole shrimp at the pond; head-on or head-off, wet or dry weight, and buyer deductions are not stated.
- No source gave a price for a size below 20 or above 100 con/kg except Tepbac 150 con/kg = 64,000 (last quote 09/10/2026).
